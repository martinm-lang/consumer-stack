## Not yet researched — the rest of the second wave

**Highest value for Zimo, in order:**

1. **Dropbox** — the storage referral is the canonical *extrinsic* referral loop and the natural
   contrast to Venmo's intrinsic one. Directly informs whether Zimo should ever pay for referrals.
2. **Facebook** — school-by-school gating, Ivy sequencing, and above all **how they removed the
   gate**. Zimo's campus-to-mainstream transition has no better precedent.
3. **BeReal** — campus ambassadors, paid referral ambassadors, the synchronized notification, and
   *why growth slowed*. The ambassador economics are the most directly comparable to a Zimo campus
   launch.
4. **Yik Yak** — the anonymous campus app that collapsed. The counterfactual Fizz was built against.
5. **Robinhood** — waitlist ranking and position-in-line mechanics; the counterexample to
   "hide the number" (LAWS Law 7).
6. **Locket** — widget distribution + TikTok UGC; the closest comparable to Airbuds' widget play.
7. **Musical.ly / TikTok** — the watermark loop; the best artifact-distribution mechanic in
   existence. (`knowledge/alex-zhu.md` covers Zhu's *thinking*; there is no case file.)
8. **Poparazzi** — pre-launch hype, App Store preorder, launch-day compression, and whether hype
   converted to retention. A pure spike case.
9. **Clubhouse** — invite scarcity and high-status seeding; the definitive "explosive growth,
   no durability" case.
10. **Instagram, Discord, Airbnb, Pinterest** — all valuable, none campus- or money-adjacent.

## Method notes for the next pass

- **University newspapers are the highest-value under-used source for campus-era stories.** They are
  contemporaneous, they carry launch-day operational detail national outlets skip, and they quote
  the student critics nobody else quotes. The Stanford Daily's week-one Fizz figure and the Daily
  Pennsylvanian's 2009 Venmo piece were each worth more than any secondary retrospective.
- **Academic ethics case studies are excellent and under-searched.** The Stanford McCoy Center's
  Fizz study was the single best source in this pass, with primary citations throughout.
- **State Attorney General settlements are primary legal documents about growth mechanics.** The NY
  AG's Saturn action documents verification, contact-book and ambassador-disclosure practices no
  interview would ever surface. **Search for these routinely** — for any consumer app serving minors,
  check state AG actions first.
- **Two Tier A YouTube searches returned nothing usable** (Saturn under one phrasing, Airbuds
  entirely). Absence of a podcast is not absence of a story — pivot to trade press.
- Three transcripts pulled in this pass were **biographical and low-yield**: `lBnFXYNO9gs` and
  `PAH8O7XOMTk` (Venmo), `SS8Eqr82390` (Whitney Wolfe, Bumble-era). Logged in their case files so
  they aren't re-pulled.
