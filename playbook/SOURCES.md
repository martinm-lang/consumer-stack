## Primary — founder writing & legal documents

- Kortina, A., ***Origins of Venmo*** — https://kortina.nyc/essays/origins-of-venmo/
  *The single best primary source in the pass: SMS mechanics, the payment note, `#p`, and the
  decision to hide amounts.*
- **New York State Attorney General**, *Settlement with App Developer for Failing to Protect Young
  Users' Privacy* (Saturn Technologies), March 2025 —
  https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2025/attorney-general-james-announces-settlement-app-developer-failing-protect-young
- **State of New York v. Saturn Technologies — Assurance of Discontinuance**, 2025 —
  https://ag.ny.gov/sites/default/files/settlements-agreements/state-of-new-york-v-saturn-technologies-assurance-of-discontinuance-2025.pdf
- **Facebook Newsroom**, *Hello. tbh, We're Moving On*, July 2018 —
  https://about.fb.com/news/2018/07/hello-tbh-moving-on/
- Airbuds Ambassadors Program — https://airbuds-fm.webflow.io/

## University newspapers — the highest-value under-used source

- **The Daily Pennsylvanian**, "Text your bill to Venmo," Dec 2009 —
  https://www.thedp.com/article/2009/12/text_your_bill_to_venmo
  *The only contemporaneous record of Venmo's campus era: the `penn-dp` invite code, campus marketer
  Harish Venkatesan, the four food trucks.*
- **The Stanford Daily**, "What's the 'buzz' at Stanford?", Aug 8 2021 — the 700-user week-one figure
  that contradicts the founder's retelling
- **The Stanford Daily**, "The Fizz phenomenon," Jan 24 2024 — Cofer's 500-campus restraint quote
- **The Stanford Daily**, "Fizz secures $4.5 million," Oct 5 2022
- **The Amherst Student**, "Fizz captures attention, draws criticism," Mar 1 2023 — 15 moderators,
  ~$500/month
- **The Occidental**, "Fizz sets campus abuzz," Feb 7 2024 — ambassador recruitment and training,
  volunteer moderators
- **Seattle Spectator**, "The problem with Fizz," May 17 2023 — meme-page outreach
- **The GW Hatchet**, Sep 29 2025; **Campus Echo** (NC Central); **Old Gold & Black** (Wake Forest);
  **The Setonian** (Seton Hall); **The Student Life** (Claremont); **The Fairfield Mirror**;
  **Colby Echo** — documented harms

## Trade & business press

**Fizz** — TechCrunch ×3 (Oct 2022 · Aug 2023 · Oct 2024), plus the Sidechat suit (Oct 2023);
Fast Company; Forbes profile
**Venmo** — TechCrunch (Braintree acquisition, Aug 16 2012); VatorNews, "When Venmo was young"
(month-by-month chronology, the two-weeks-from-bankruptcy detail, the June 2012 social-feed
redesign date); Business of Apps; Venmo Help Center (claim mechanics)
**Saturn** — Forbes (Aug 2021, $44M); Built In NYC; Entrepreneur; TechCrunch (Snap acquisition,
Jun 2025); ABC 33/40 (parent and school warnings)
**tbh / Gas** — TechCrunch (Oct 2017 · Jan 2023); Bloomberg; Variety; Washington Post;
Appfigures (installs, revenue, 30K/hour, App Store rank); Wikipedia (*Gas (app)*, shutdown date)
**Partiful** — CNBC (Apr 2025); TechCrunch (Google Play Best App 2024); Modern Retail (a16z Tech
Week mandate); Twilio Signal 2025 (SMS/RCS architecture); Sacra, Crunchbase, PitchBook
**Tinder** — Entrepreneur (Joe Munoz first-hand account); TechCrunch (Wolfe v. Tinder, Jul 2014);
Texas Monthly
**Zenly** — The Pragmatic Engineer (*Inside the Shutdown of Zenly by Snap*); Rest of World (*The
Zenly implosion*); TechCrunch (Dec 2022); Sifted
**Snapchat** — Fortune (Dec 2013, the Orange County origin and blocked-Facebook iPads); VatorNews
(*When Snapchat was young*); TechCrunch

  paywalled; thesis noted, evidence unverified

  Entrepreneur/Munoz account; cited once, at source
- **d3.harvard.edu student case studies on Venmo** — student-written; used only for orientation
- Assorted growth blogs repeating the tbh 40% figure — treated as one source (Bier), not many
